Questions

Who reads the sponsor segment?

The narrator who voices the whole video, so the read sounds like part of the show.

How is the price set?

Expected views per video multiplied by the rate for that market and placement. Prices update when a channel grows.

What if the video gets fewer views than expected?

If a video is under 70% of expected views after 30 days, we run your read again in the next video at no cost.

What if the video can't go live?

We move your read to the next open date on the same channel, or to a similar channel, or refund you in full. You choose.

Can I cancel?

Full refund up to 7 days before the upload date. After you approve the script, the slot is final.
